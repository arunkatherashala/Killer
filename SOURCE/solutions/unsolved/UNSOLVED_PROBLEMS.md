### 🟡 HARD MATHEMATICAL PROBLEMS (Likely Unsolvable)

#### **1. Collatz Conjecture**
- **Status**: ❌ Still UNSOLVED (60+ years, tested to 2^68)
- **Problem**: Does every positive integer reach 1via n→n/2 (if even) or 3n+1 (if odd)?

  - Exhibits chaotic behavior (no pattern found)
- **KILLER Confidence**: 85% (Almost certainly true, but proof unknown)

#### **2. Goldbach's Conjecture**
- **Status**: ❌ UNPROVEN (286 years, since 1742)

- **Why KILLER can't solve**:

- **KILLER Confidence**: 98% (Extremely confident it's true, but no proof)

  - Prime distribution remains mysterious
