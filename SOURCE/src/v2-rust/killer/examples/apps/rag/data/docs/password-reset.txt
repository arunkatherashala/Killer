Resetting your password

To reset your password open the Settings page, choose Security and press the Reset password button. A reset link is then sent to your registered email address. The link expires after one hour, so use it promptly. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and make sure you typed the same address you used to sign up.

Choose a new password with at least twelve characters. Passwords that appear in public breach lists are rejected. After the change every active session is signed out and you must log in again on each device.

If you no longer have access to the registered email address, contact support and be ready to verify your identity with a government issued photo ID.
