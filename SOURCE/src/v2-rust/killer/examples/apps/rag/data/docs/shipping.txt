Shipping and delivery

Orders ship within two business days. Standard delivery takes five to seven days, express delivery takes two days and is available for most addresses in Europe and North America. Tracking numbers are emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse.

Customs duties and import taxes are not included in the price for destinations outside the European Union. The carrier may contact you to collect them before delivery. A parcel that cannot be delivered after three attempts is returned to the warehouse, and the shipping fee is not refunded.

If a parcel is damaged on arrival, take photos of the box and the contents and send them to support within seven days. We will ship a replacement or refund the item.
